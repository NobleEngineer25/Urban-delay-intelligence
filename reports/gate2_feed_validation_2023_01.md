## Method

- **Geocoding:** city-supplied lat/lon is kept when it falls inside the Chicago bounding box (lat (41.6, 42.1), lon (-88.0, -87.5)). Records without it are matched offline to the nearest known house number on the same street (within 200 numbers, about 400 m), using every raw 311 and permit file as the address reference.
- **H3:** every geocoded record is tagged with its resolution-8 cell (output: `data/processed/disruptions_h3_YYYY_MM.parquet`).
- **Duplicates:** 311 requests the city flags as duplicates are excluded before every check and rate above.
- **Community areas:** 311 records carry one and are graded. Permits borrow the most common 311 community area in the same H3 cell, so their coverage is reported (INFO) but not graded.
- **Temporal:** counts new records per day (311 created date, permit start date), so a feed that stops arriving shows empty days. Permits are graded on weekdays only.
